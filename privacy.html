Privacy Policy

Fee Hisab · Last updated: 10 November 2026

Overview

Fee Hisab is a fee-management app for schools, coaching institutes and tuition teachers. It works offline and has no account of its own.

Data we handle

Institute name, student names, roll numbers, parent and student phone numbers, addresses, batches, fees, payments, expenses and receipts that you type into the app.

Where data is stored

On your own device (browser storage), encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key protected by your 6-digit PIN. We do not run a server, and we do not sell your data.

Google Drive backup (optional)

Google Drive backup is optional and is off until the owner signs in with Google.

When connected, the app asks Google for permission to use only a hidden app-data folder in your own Drive (drive.appdata) and to read your email address to show which account is connected. It cannot see your other Drive files.

Backup files are encrypted on your device with a key protected by your PIN before they are uploaded. We have no server and cannot read them. Google stores them under your account.

While the app is open and signed in, it saves a backup about every 6 hours and when you tap Back up now. It keeps the latest copy and the last 7 daily copies. Google sign-in access lasts about one hour, so if it has expired the app asks you to tap Sync now. Backups do not run while the app is closed.

To restore, choose Restore from Drive and enter the Owner PIN. Without the PIN that was active when the backup was made, a backup cannot be opened.

To stop: tap Disconnect in the app, and remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete the backups, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Fee Hisab → Delete hidden app data.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide backup and restore and is never sold or used for advertising.

Other sharing

When you tap Send on WhatsApp, Share or Share Backup, the text or file is handed to the app you choose, by your own action. Their policies then apply.

Advertising

We may show advertisements in future versions of the app. Before that happens we will update this policy and describe what data any ad provider uses.

Permissions

The app does not need camera, location, microphone or contacts permissions.

Your control and deletion

You can export your data (encrypted backup, CSV) at any time. To delete data on the device, clear the site data of this app or uninstall it. To delete Drive backups, see the Google Drive section above. If you lose your PIN, data can be recovered only from a backup file.

Children

The app is meant for institute owners and staff. Student details are entered by the institute, which is responsible for using them lawfully, including under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
