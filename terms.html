Google login and Drive backup

Signing in with Google is optional and is used only for backup and restore.

When connected, the app asks Google for permission to use only a hidden app-data folder in your own Drive (drive.appdata) and to read your email address to show which account is connected. It cannot see your other Drive files.

Backup files are encrypted on your device with a key protected by your PIN before they are uploaded. We have no server and cannot read them. Google stores them under your account.

While the app is open and signed in, it saves a backup about every 6 hours and when you tap Back up now. It keeps the latest copy and the last 7 daily copies. Google sign-in access lasts about one hour, so if it has expired the app asks you to tap Sync now. Backups do not run while the app is closed.

To restore, choose Restore from Drive and enter the Owner PIN. Without the PIN that was active when the backup was made, a backup cannot be opened.

To stop: tap Disconnect in the app, and remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete the backups, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Fee Hisab → Delete hidden app data.

Backups depend on Google services and your internet connection. We do not guarantee that a backup will always succeed, so check the last backup time in More → Google Drive. Google’s own terms apply to your Google account and Drive storage.
